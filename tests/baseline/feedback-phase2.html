Feedback from Phase II teams

EBiM Benchmark Competition 2026

This questionnaire closed on 5 November 2026. Thank you to everyone who answered.

This questionnaire is for everyone on a team that took part in Phase II of the EBiM Benchmark Competition 2026. Above all, we want to understand why policies that worked in simulation did or did not work on the real robots, and what would make them work. Only the people who built them can tell us. It takes about 20 minutes, or about 30 if you built your team's policy. Every question is optional, except a few details that some choices need. One response per person, please. It closes on 5 November 2026 (AoE).

Your answers are read only by the EBiM organizers, reported only in aggregate, and never quoted or attributed to you without your permission. Results are final, and nothing here can affect them.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back later.

To answer in 中文, switch language before you start. Your answers so far are kept.

Section 8 · Working with us on the analysis

We want to understand, team by team, why policies that worked in simulation did or didn't work on the real robots, and to work out with you what would make them work. We don't have your training code or data, so we can only do this together. Everything here is optional and is only a statement of interest: nothing is shared until we have agreed the details with your team. Anything you share is used only for this analysis and is published only with your agreement.
